# The package's content half — what is carried and what is written

Generated by [`tools/assemble_content.py`](tools/assemble_content.py) from the
ATM10 jar `ports/_shared/licence-cache/sushigocrafting-1.21-0.6.7.jar`
(**MIT**, read from `META-INF/neoforge.mods.toml`; rank 0). Rerun the script to
rebuild: it deletes `assets/sushigocrafting/` and `data/` first, then copies
from the jar and writes the declarations, so the output is reproducible.

**Where the files live (SDK layout, 0.2.0):** `assets/` and `data/` are at the
**package root**, beside `src/` and the one `horizon-mod.toml` — there is no
`content/` folder and no content-only manifest any more. The 0.1.0 tree under
`ports/_shared/evidence/sushigocrafting/retired-hnmod-0.1.0/` is the historical
copy: the 61 files are byte-identical (`notes.md`, "Migration to the SDK format").

## Deliberately not carried (gaps, see `notes.md`)

- The **six cutting-board recipes** (`data/sushigocrafting/recipe/cutting_board/*.json`)
  are the mod's **own recipe type** (`sushigocrafting:cutting_board`), which
  Horizon cannot register; the same six rows live in `runtime.rs::RECIPES`, unit
  tested against the JSON files, and the port's readout shows the row a board
  would cut.
- The five **crops**, the **avocado tree**, the two **fish entities**, the three
  **mob effects**, the **rice cooker / fermentation barrel / roller / cooler box**,
  the **sushi foods** and the roller's weighing UI, the **seed loot modifiers**,
  the **worldgen and biome modifiers** and the **Patchouli book**: other slices.
- The cutting board's **client renderer** (`CuttingBoardRenderer` draws the item
  on the board and the knife animation) and its GUI (there is none upstream).
